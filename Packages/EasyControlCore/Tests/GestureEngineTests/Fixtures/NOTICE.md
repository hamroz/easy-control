# Fixture notice

`pose-fixture.jsonl` contains 306 hand-feature vectors derived from images of **HaGRID — HAnd Gesture Recognition
Image Dataset** (https://github.com/hukenovs/hagrid), licensed under a public licence based on CC BY-SA 4.0
(https://github.com/hukenovs/hagrid/blob/master/license/en_us.pdf). Changes: landmarks re-detected with Apple
Vision, converted into feature vectors, and labels remapped to EasyControl's pose classes.
